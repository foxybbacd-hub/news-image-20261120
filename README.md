## The image

A newspaper front page, drawn entirely in code (`poster.html`, rendered with headless Chromium) and set in the Inter typeface. It leads with the day's biggest story — the first-ever meeting of the American, Chinese and Russian leaders in Shenzhen and their "peace declaration" — and covers four more, across regions and fields:

1. **Three powers, one table** — Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen and sign a "peace declaration" promising talks on nuclear risks.
2. **World court: Myanmar guilty of genocide** — the ICJ finds Myanmar responsible for genocide against the Rohingya, the first time a state has been held to account.
3. **Cyclone Akvan strikes India's east coast** — landfall near Gopalpur, Odisha, with winds of 90–100 km/h and more than 70,000 people evacuated.
4. **Van Gogh sells for a record $190.4m** — "Blossoming Chestnut Trees" sets an artist's record at Sotheby's in New York.
5. **Nicholls' century puts New Zealand on top** — Henry Nicholls finishes day one unbeaten on 113 against India in Wellington.

The program (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`. The image is 1024×1024 pixels.
